# Thomas Vincent

Ina gina kayan aiki don tafiyar da ababen more rayuwa na fasaha (infrastructure), sarrafa ayyukan macOS ta atomatik, da sauƙaƙa binciken yadda tsari ke aiki. Aikina ya haɗa da Go, Python, Rust, PHP, TypeScript, da saita ababen more rayuwa na fasaha.

## Zaɓaɓɓun ayyuka

| Aiki | Abin da yake samarwa |
| --- | --- |
| [Tsarin ababen more rayuwa na GitHub runner](https://github.com/somethingwithproof/github-runners-infra) | Mai sarrafawa na Go don masu gudanar da aiki (runners) masu aiki guda ɗaya a iyakar ma'ajiya a kan DigitalOcean, AWS, Google Cloud, da Azure | |
| [California Solar Atlas](https://github.com/somethingwithproof/california-solar-atlas) | Mai binciken bayanai mai tsaye (static) don bayanan wutar lantarkin hasken rana da aka rarraba a California, tare da tsarin bincike da bayyanannun iyakokin ɗauka da ƙirar lissafi | |
| [MacGTD](https://github.com/somethingwithproof/MacGTD) | Tsarin tattara bayanai na macOS don manhajojin haɓaka ƙwazo, ta amfani da AppleScript, Automator, da Alfred | |
| [CommitKit](https://github.com/somethingwithproof/commitkit-rust) | Kayan aikin layin umarni na Rust mai hulɗa don gina saƙonnin Conventional Commit | |
| [Binciken farashin Bitcoin](https://github.com/somethingwithproof/check_bitcoin_price) | Ƙarin manhaja na Nagios/Icinga mai iyakokin farashi, binciken sabuntar farashi na zaɓi, da iyakantaccen sake gwada buƙata | |

README na kowane aiki, tushen lamba, da sakamakon tsarin aiki suna bayyana iyakarsa ta yanzu. Fasalolin gwaji da haɗin kai na musamman ga dandamali suna buƙatar nasu tantancewar; lamba (badge) ba da'awar shiryawa don aikin zahiri ba ce.

## Sauran ayyuka

- **Sa ido kan hanyar sadarwa:** [Kadupul](https://github.com/kadupulhq/kadupul), aikin sa ido da aka samo daga Cacti ƙarƙashin ƙungiyar `kadupulhq`.
- **Saita ababen more rayuwa na fasaha:** Sarrafa [MariaDB](https://github.com/somethingwithproof/ansible-role-mariadb), [WordPress](https://github.com/somethingwithproof/ansible-wordpress-enterprise), da [firewall ta na'ura](https://github.com/somethingwithproof/ansible-collection-firewall) ta atomatik, tare da iyakokin aiwatarwa na musamman da aka rubuta a kowace ma'ajiya.
- **Haɗin kai da macOS:** [Sabobin MCP](https://github.com/somethingwithproof/mcp-servers), haɗaɗɗiyar ma'ajiya don haɗin kan manhajoji.
- **Kayan aikin CI:** [Foundry](https://github.com/somethingwithproof/foundry), mai gudanar da umarni bisa bayanin martaba (profile) tare da tsara abubuwan dogaro da rajistar aiwatarwa.

Ina kuma aiki a kan tsarin binciken shaidar zuriyya da binciken bayanan kasuwa. Waɗannan abubuwan sha'awa ba sa nufin tabbataccen sakamakon bincike mai takardar shaida, ingantaccen sakamakon ciniki, ko kasancewar kowace ma'ajiyar bincike a buɗe ga jama'a.

## Rahotannin tsaro

Bi fayil ɗin `SECURITY.md` na ma'ajiyar da abin ya shafa da hanyar ba da rahoto ta sirri da aka saita. Ga ayyukan tushe (upstream), yi amfani da tsarin bayyana matsaloli na aikin tushe. Guji buɗe matsalolin jama'a da ke ɗauke da bayanan raunin tsaro da ba a bayyana ba ko takardun shaidar shiga.
